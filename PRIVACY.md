# Privacy Policy

**NexusKestrel** — AutomataNexus, LLC
Last updated: 2026-11-14

This policy explains what a NexusKestrel controller and the NexusEdge Console collect
and why.

## 1. What the controller sends

When connected to the NexusEdge Console, a controller sends, over an encrypted
connection:

- **Facility Data**: point values, trends, alarms, equipment status, schedules, and
  the commands and overrides applied to your equipment;
- **Device health**: firmware version, uptime, network status, protocol counters,
  and error logs;
- **Configuration**: the controller's settings and its integration sheet (the list of
  devices and points it serves).

The controller does not collect personal information about building occupants. It has
no camera or microphone. Occupancy inputs, where wired, are reported as equipment
data (occupied / unoccupied), not as identities.

## 2. What the Console stores about people

For Console users we store name, e-mail address, organization, role, sign-in records,
and an audit record of the commands each user sends to equipment. Command audit
records are kept because they are part of operating equipment safely.

## 3. How we use it

- to operate the Console: display, trend, alarm, and command your equipment;
- to send the alarm notifications you configure;
- to support and update your controllers;
- to train fault-detection models **for your own units** from their own operating
  history, and, in de-identified form, to improve our products.

We do not sell Facility Data or personal information, and we do not use it for
advertising.

## 4. Where it is kept

Facility Data is stored on AutomataNexus-operated infrastructure and with service
providers we contract with to run the Console. Access is limited to AutomataNexus
staff, those providers as needed to run the service, and the people you authorize.

## 5. Retention

Facility Data and audit records are kept for the life of your subscription and as
your agreement provides after it ends, then deleted. Ask us for an export or for
deletion at any time.

## 6. Operating without the Console

A controller can run its local control without any Console connection. In that mode
it sends nothing to AutomataNexus.
